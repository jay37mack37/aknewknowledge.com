Question / remember / play

Change your
perspective.

Press Start to load the game. It uses WebGL and plays sound once you interact; you can mute it any time.

The game loads only when you press Start.

01 / Move

Keyboard or touch.

W or ↑ moves forward. A / D or ← / → turn at the next opening. S or ↓ turns around. On touch screens, use the on-screen arrows.

02 / Pause

Take a breath.

P or Esc pauses and resumes; the Pause button does too. From the pause screen you can restart. The game also pauses when you switch tabs.

03 / Change your view

See it differently.

C or Q cycles first-person, chase and front views. If your system asks for reduced motion, camera bob and shake are switched off.

While you play, your keys go to the game. Tab reaches the game's own buttons; “Close game” below it brings you back here. Open the game on its own page ↗
