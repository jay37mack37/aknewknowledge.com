Threader Drive

Threader is a private development and AI workspace operated by the owner of A Knew Knowledge. Its Google Drive connections let the operator access their authorized accounts from their own computers using rclone.

What the connection does

With the account holder's permission, rclone mounts Google Drive as a local filesystem. It can list, read, create, change and delete files when the operator or a program they authorize requests those actions.

The Google consent screens currently identify these connections as Project Threadripper Backup and Threadripper Backup 2. These are the existing OAuth application names used for Threader's Drive mounts and related personal backup connections.

Private access

This is a personal tool for accounts approved by the operator. This website does not offer public sign-up, receive Google authorization tokens, or provide access to the mounted files.

Read the Google Drive privacy policy for information about permissions, local storage, retention and revoking access.
