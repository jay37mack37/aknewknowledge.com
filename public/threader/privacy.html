Threader Drive privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Google Drive connections used by Threader through rclone, including the existing Google OAuth applications named Project Threadripper Backup and Threadripper Backup 2. The operator is the owner of the A Knew Knowledge environment.

Google data accessed and purpose

The connections access the authorized account's email address for identity checks, Drive file and folder metadata, file contents requested by the operator, and storage information. Full Google Drive permission allows listing, reading, creating, modifying and deleting files. Those permissions support filesystem mounts, operator-directed file operations and personal backups; they do not grant other website visitors access.

Storage and protection

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in private rclone configuration on the operator's computers. Requested file contents may be held in local filesystem caches, local copies or backups. Operational logs may contain filenames and error details. Access is controlled by operating-system account permissions. Google API traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections. This static website does not receive Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Use and sharing

Google data is used to provide these personal file-access and backup functions. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. The mount does not automatically send Drive contents to AI providers. Any separate processing or sharing must be explicitly directed by the account holder and follow that service's applicable privacy terms. Access by the operator or their administrators is limited to operating, securing and troubleshooting the environment.

Threader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained while the account connection is enabled. Local caches are managed by rclone's cache settings; open files and pending uploads can be retained longer than ordinary cache limits. Local copies, backups and logs have their own retention and can remain after the mount is removed. Revoking authorization does not itself delete existing copies.

The account holder can ask the operator to remove a connection, its saved credentials and retained local data. Pending uploads must be resolved before removing their cached data. Cloud files are deleted only when the account holder directs a deletion; uninstalling a mount does not delete the Google Drive account.

Control and revocation

Account holders can revoke the application's access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access after Google invalidates the credentials.

Website and contact

These Threader pages contain no analytics scripts, sign-in forms or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary request information under GitHub's privacy statement. For access, deletion or privacy questions, contact jarrod.womack@aknewknowledge.com.

Threader is a private, owner-operated environment. Contact the operator.
